Why Premarital Screening Matters

Genetic Awareness

Premarital genetic screening helps identify inherited disorders such as thalassemia and sickle cell disease before marriage.

Healthy Future

Early health testing helps couples make informed healthcare decisions and reduce future medical risks.

Disease Prevention

Screening for HIV, Hepatitis, and other infections improves long-term health safety for both partners.

Disease Awareness

Thalassemia

A genetic blood disorder that reduces hemoglobin production. Premarital screening helps prevent transmission to children.

Sickle Cell Disease

An inherited disorder causing abnormal red blood cells and severe health complications.

HIV/AIDS

HIV weakens the immune system. Early testing and awareness improve prevention and treatment.

Hepatitis B

A liver infection spread through blood and body fluids. Screening and vaccination are highly important.
